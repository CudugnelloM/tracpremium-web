Account Deletion Policy

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Temporary deactivation or suspension is not the same as deleting the account and associated data.

Request deletion by email

Never send your password. We may verify that you control the email address or phone number linked to the account.

1. How to request deletion

  • In the app: Profile → Security & Settings → Delete account.
  • On the public page: https://cudugnellom.github.io/tracpremium-web/pages/AccountDeletion.html.
  • By email: admin@tracpremium.com.
  • Never send your password by email or through support.

2. Identity verification

To prevent unauthorised requests, we may require reauthentication, a code sent to the verified email or phone already linked to the account or another reasonable check. We only request additional data that is necessary and proportionate to verify control of the account.

3. Data erased or anonymised

  • Profile, credentials, sessions and authentication tokens.
  • Notification tokens and preferences no longer needed.
  • Contact details and profile photograph.
  • Verification documents that do not have to be retained.
  • Location and technical data that is no longer needed.
  • Private content and other account-linked data not subject to lawful retention.
  • Processors will be instructed to erase data in accordance with applicable instructions and contracts.

4. Data that may remain blocked

Some data may remain blocked, with access and use restricted, for the time needed to meet legal, tax, accounting or regulatory duties; resolve payments, trips, bookings or claims; prevent fraud or abuse; investigate security; defend rights; or respond to authorities.

Blocked data is not used for advertising, new transactions or ordinary purposes. It is destroyed or anonymised when the applicable period ends.

Backups may temporarily retain data until secure rotation or overwriting.

5. Content involving other people

Messages received by another person, trip records, invoices, transactions, proof and ratings may be retained where needed for other parties, legal duties, transaction integrity or fraud prevention. Where possible, identity is removed or replaced and associated public content is withdrawn.

6. Subscriptions and pending transactions

Deleting the account does not automatically cancel a subscription managed by Google Play, Apple App Store, Stripe or another external provider. Review and cancel through the provider where necessary.

We may identify pending transactions, payments or claims but will not unjustifiably obstruct the right to erasure. Pending duties are handled using the minimum blocked data necessary.

7. Timing and confirmation

We acknowledge and respond to the request without undue delay and generally within one month. Complex or numerous requests may require up to two additional months; we will explain this within the first month.

Once the request is verified and processed, confirmation is sent to a verified email address or phone. If some data cannot be erased, we explain the retention basis and available complaint routes.

8. Controller and contact

Controller: Andrés Cudugnello, a natural person who owns the TracPremium brand in Spain. Email: admin@tracpremium.com. Website: https://cudugnellom.github.io/tracpremium-web/.

You may complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency, https://www.aepd.es, or another competent supervisory authority.
