TracPremium Privacy Policy

This Policy explains how personal data is processed through the TracPremium app, website, support channels and platform. It applies according to the user’s role, the features used and the specific relationship with the platform.

1. Controller and scope

Controller: Andrés Cudugnello, a natural person who owns the TracPremium brand in Spain. Privacy email: admin@tracpremium.com. Website: https://cudugnellom.github.io/tracpremium-web/. Country of establishment: Spain.

This Policy applies to carriers, drivers, load generators or owners, loading companies, warehouse owners or managers, representatives, TracCooperativo users, support contacts and other authorised participants.

TracPremium is a brand. The controller is Andrés Cudugnello, the responsible natural person and owner of the TracPremium brand, not a separate legal entity.

2. Account, identity and contact data

  • Name, surname, username, email, telephone, address and country.
  • Language, preferences, profile photograph and internal identifiers.
  • Account creation, access and change dates; verification, restriction, suspension and deletion status.
  • Credentials protected by cryptographic hashing; TracPremium should not be able to read the original password.
  • Trade or company name, tax identifiers, representative, business or tax address and billing data.

3. Professional verification, vehicles, loads and warehouses

  • Verification selfie or photograph; ID card, foreigner ID, passport; driving licence; professional competence, transport, tachograph, ADR, ATP and equivalent documents.
  • Licence plate, make, model, type, capacity, dimensions, trailer, technical documents, roadworthiness, insurance, availability and operating areas.
  • Origin, destination, collection, delivery, dates, goods, weight, volume, pallets, requirements, bids, bookings, assignments, incidents, QR, waybills, proof and trip history.
  • Warehouse address, location, title or authority, photographs, capacity, area, licences, insurance, certificates, availability and bookings.
  • Full official documents are not displayed publicly. If a selfie is technically processed to uniquely identify a person, biometric data is processed and a separate notice will explain the Article 9 GDPR condition and safeguards.

4. Location

We may process approximate or precise location to show nearby resources, confirm collection or delivery points, calculate routes, navigate, track active trips, document incidents, publish geolocated contributions and provide safety or SOS features.

Precise background location is collected only when the user activates a feature that needs it and the operating system grants the relevant permission. Tracking must stop when the trip ends, the feature is disabled or the data is no longer needed.

Location is not sold or used for personalised advertising. Withdrawing permission may disable nearby results, navigation, tracking or SOS.

5. Photographs, files, chat and content

  • Photographs and files selected or captured for profiles, verification, vehicles, loads, warehouses, waybills, delivery proof, chat, TracCooperativo or incidents.
  • Messages, sender, recipient, date, delivery or read status and attachments. Chat is not necessarily end-to-end encrypted.
  • Contributions, comments, votes, reports, ratings, location and moderation status in TracCooperativo.
  • Support and administrator communications and reports of fraud, abuse or improper behaviour.
  • Where available, the app uses the system picker and does not indiscriminately access the entire photo library.

6. SOS, payments, device and local biometrics

  • For SOS: identity, precise location, emergency type, date, trip, vehicle, messages and people receiving or responding to the alert. Necessary information may be shared with nearby users, assistance providers or authorities.
  • For payments: customer and transaction identifiers, amount, currency, status, plan, dates, billing, refunds, fraud results and partial payment-method details supplied by the provider.
  • Technical data: IP, device, operating system, version, language, time zone, installation identifiers, push token, connections, authentication, failures, performance, features used and security signals.
  • Fingerprint or face data used for local unlock is processed by the operating system. TracPremium receives the authentication result and does not store the device’s biometric template.

7. Data sources

Data may come from the user, their company or representative, other transaction participants, the device and granted permissions, technical or payment providers, lawfully accessible public sources and authorised staff verifying documents or handling incidents.

A person providing third-party data must have sufficient authority or another legal basis and must inform the third party where required.

8. Purposes and legal bases

  • Contract or pre-contractual steps: create and manage accounts; authenticate; connect users; manage loads, warehouses, bids, bookings, trips, chat, documents, payments, support and requested features.
  • Legal obligation: invoicing and accounting; authority requests; blocking erased data; and other applicable tax, judicial, regulatory and safety duties.
  • Legitimate interests: prevent fraud and abuse, protect the platform, investigate incidents, keep proportionate technical logs, improve stability, moderate content and establish or defend claims. The required balancing test will be performed and users may object.
  • Consent: optional or background location where consent is the appropriate basis, non-essential marketing and other identified optional features. Consent can be withdrawn without affecting prior lawful processing.
  • Operating-system permission for camera, files, notifications or location enables technical access but is not by itself a GDPR legal basis.

9. Required data and visibility to users

Required fields are identified in each form. Not providing them may prevent account creation or verification, vehicle or warehouse registration, participation in a transaction, tracking or SOS, purchases or document compliance.

Depending on the feature, other users may see a name, photograph, role, rating, professional information, load or warehouse details, offer or trip status, necessary operational location, sent messages, public contributions or information needed for an alert.

Passwords, authentication codes, full official documents and full payment details are not displayed publicly.

10. Providers and recipients

We may use Google Cloud Platform for infrastructure and storage; Firebase for notifications and technical services; Google Maps Platform and HERE Technologies for maps, routing and navigation; Stripe and app stores for payments and subscriptions; Vonage or similar communications providers; and email, security, monitoring, diagnostics and support providers.

Recipients may also include users where necessary for a transaction; confidential advisers; insurers or assistance providers; a buyer in a reorganisation subject to safeguards; and administrative, judicial or law-enforcement authorities where a legal basis exists.

Providers act in the legal role applicable to them. Processors are bound by contract and may only process documented and necessary data.

11. International transfers

Some providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where applicable, we rely on an adequacy decision — including the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework for certified recipients — Standard Contractual Clauses, supplementary measures or another valid mechanism.

You may request information about the mechanism and a copy or reference to the applicable safeguards by emailing admin@tracpremium.com.

12. Retention, erasure and blocking

  • Account and profile: while active and, after closure, only for the time strictly needed to process erasure, subject to data that must be blocked.
  • Transactions, contracts, trips, bookings and claims: for the relationship and the statutory limitation periods applying to the transaction or dispute.
  • Billing, accounting and tax records: for the periods required by commercial, tax and fraud-prevention law, including lawful interruption or extension.
  • Detailed location: only while needed for the trip, safety, an incident or a claim; it is then erased or anonymised.
  • Verification documents: while needed to verify requirements and afterwards erased or blocked where a duty or potential liability remains.
  • Technical and security logs: for the proportionate period defined in the internal retention schedule according to the risk and purpose.
  • Backups: until secure rotation or overwriting, with restricted access and no reuse for ordinary purposes.
  • In Spain, erased data that must be retained for potential liabilities is blocked under Article 32 of the LOPDGDD and destroyed when the applicable period ends.

13. Account deletion

Requests can be made through Profile → Security & Settings → Delete account, at https://cudugnellom.github.io/tracpremium-web/pages/AccountDeletion.html or by emailing admin@tracpremium.com. Never send a password by email.

Temporary deactivation or suspension is not deletion. A valid request starts permanent closure and erasure or anonymisation, subject to the retention and blocking exceptions in this Policy.

Full details appear under the “Delete account” tab.

14. Security

TracPremium applies risk-appropriate technical and organisational measures, which may include HTTPS/TLS, password hashing, authentication and authorisation, role-based access, security logging, backups, environment separation, document controls and secret protection.

No system is perfectly secure. Breaches will be investigated, contained and reported to the authority and, where required, affected people in accordance with law.

15. Automated decisions

Rules may be used to rank results, detect outstanding documents, send alerts, identify unusual activity and show operational matches.

No solely automated decision producing legal or similarly significant effects will be made without a sufficient legal basis and safeguards. Users may request human intervention and review where applicable.

16. Your rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction, portability, withdrawal of consent and review of solely automated decisions where applicable.

Email admin@tracpremium.com and identify the right and affected account. Additional information will only be requested where necessary and proportionate to verify identity. Do not send your password.

We respond without undue delay and generally within one month. This may be extended by two further months due to complexity or request volume; we will explain the extension within the first month.

You may complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency, https://www.aepd.es, or another competent supervisory authority.

17. Children

TracPremium is a professional platform and is not directed to children. An account may not be created by a person without sufficient legal or professional capacity. If an unauthorised child account is identified, it may be closed and the data handled as required by law.

18. Cookies and external services

The website must provide a Cookie Policy and consent controls where non-essential cookies or similar technologies are used. Third-party integrations and pages are also governed by their policies where they act as independent controllers.

19. Changes and contact

We will display the update date and notify relevant changes before they take effect through the app, email or a notification.

For privacy, rights or deletion: Andrés Cudugnello, admin@tracpremium.com, https://cudugnellom.github.io/tracpremium-web/.
